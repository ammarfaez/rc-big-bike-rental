The Garage

A garage that behaves like one.

RC Big Bike Rental Malaysia (RCBR) started in 2016 as a two-person repair bench. We tore down old engines, learned every torque spec, and fell in love with honest work. The rental fleet grew out of that — machines we maintain and trust as our own.

Every single bike is serviced, checked and road-tested in our own garage after every return. You don't get a machine off a rack; you get one we'd happily ride across the country ourselves.

FAQ

Fair questions. Straight answers.

What do I need to rent a motorcycle?

A valid motorcycle driving licence, a decent riding history and a refundable security deposit. Scooters are more relaxed — a car licence may be enough.

Is there a minimum age?

You must be 21+ to rent, and at least 2 years licensed riding experience is required for our larger bikes.

How much is the deposit, and when is it back?

A holding deposit is taken at booking and returned in full once the bike is back in good shape — usually within 24 hours.

Are the bikes insured?

Third-party insurance is included on every rental. You can add premium full cover for extra peace of mind on longer tours.

Do you deliver and pick up?

We deliver and pick up anywhere in the city at a small flat fee, and it's free with weekly or monthly rentals.

What if I break down on the road?

Every rental includes 24/7 roadside assistance. One call on WhatsApp and we'll get a mechanic to you or bring a replacement ride.
